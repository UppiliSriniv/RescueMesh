Traffic Separation & Reliability Protocol

  • Separate Traffic Classes: Video feeds travel over Wi-Fi; relay packets carry short telemetry and alerts only (never live video).
  • Delivery Guarantees: Application-level acknowledgements (ACKs), sequence IDs, bounded retries, and gateway duplicate suppression.
  • Outage Resilience: Store-and-forward queuing, local edge logging, and independent MCU command watchdog timeout.

Important Engineering Note on ESP-NOW

If ESP32 / ESP-NOW modules are used for low-overhead telemetry links, note that ESP-NOW does not automatically provide a multi-hop mesh. Application-level routing, hop selection, ACKs, sequence numbering, and duplicate suppression must be implemented explicitly in firmware/software.
